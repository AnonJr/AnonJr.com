My voice is a resonant baritone -- calm, conversational, and approachable, with a measured pace and strong focus on clarity.

I specialize in non-fiction, academic, and eLearning narration. My delivery is deliberate, steady, and easy to follow, making it well suited to educational content, technical instruction, complex subjects, and reflective essays where listeners benefit from a thoughtful presentation.

When I worked in Training and Development, I narrated eLearning modules and taught numerous in-person classes. Since then, I've continued developing my voice work through live-streamed projects such as [Games Revisted]({% link _pages/gamesrevisited.md %}) and [CoffeeCraft]({% link _pages/coffeecraft.md %}), and I am currently expanding my narration portfolio as an independent contractor.

My natural inclination is to dry wit and understated humor, which is why I particularly enjoyed narrating several Mark Twain essays for LibriVox.
