![Avatar](/assets/res/avatar-apron.png){: .align-left} I absolutely hate filling out "about" pages and various profiles. I never know what to put. I do know I won't succumb to the usual "speak about myself in the 3rd person" style of entry.

Do I put what I do here? I'm more than my job.

I am a Christian. It definitely shapes my perspective, feelings, and outlook. I love learning, and sharing that learning. I like making things - physical things with my hands, software, whatever - and I like figuring out how things work, being able to not just know but understand from a first-principles foundation.

I've done a lot of jobs. I've been a programmer, coach, instructor, luthier, lifeguard, machine operator, and various other things. Wherever I end up, I tend towards the "Jack of all Trades" style employee. The full quote, not the misleading fragment everyone remembers.

At this juncture, I'm trying to get back to writing - this time in public. I did a fair amount before, but it was all behind the company firewall and not really for public consumption. I'm also launching a [podcast]({% link _pages/biblebyexample.md %}) and re-starting [Games Revisited]({% link _pages/gamesrevisited.md %}) after it's protracted hiatus. I'm available for [voice work]({% link _pages/voicework.md %}) too if you'd like to rent this beautiful baritone. 😉
