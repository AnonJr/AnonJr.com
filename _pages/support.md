## How Your Support Is Used

I believe supporters deserve to know where their money goes. While I won't be posting quarterly accounting statements, here's a general overview of how support for *AnonJr* is handled behind the scenes. These priorities may evolve as the projects grow, but this is the general approach I intend to follow.

### Tithes

The first 10% of every dollar received is set aside as a tithe. I don't expect everyone who supports this work to share my faith, but I want everyone to know where that portion goes.

The tithe currently supports [Wells of Living Water](https://www.wellsoflivingwater.org/), a ministry I know personally and am happy to support. Their work serves communities in Peru by drilling wells, providing access to potable water, and sharing the Gospel.

### Operating Costs and Infrastructure

After the tithe, support helps cover the ordinary costs of running and maintaining these projects, including:

- Federal, state, and local taxes.
- Domain registration and website hosting.
- Media hosting and distribution.
- Business software and administrative services.
- Payment processing fees.
- Equipment maintenance and replacement.

### Reinvestment

Whatever remains after tithes, taxes, and operating expenses is reinvested in the work itself. That may mean acquiring games and other resources for future articles and episodes, upgrading equipment, or helping offset the time spent researching, writing, recording, editing, and maintaining AnonJr.com and its related projects.
